## Brand

Use logo-1024.png for most app and product placements; logo-2048.png is the largest export. The navy tile has transparent rounded corners. Use profile-400.png for X, or profile-1024.png as a reusable profile master. Wordmark PNGs are transparent; choose on-light or on-dark to match the destination background. Keep the mark proportions intact and leave space around it.

Navy: #151b31. Paper: #f2f2f2. Mint: #A8DCC4. Amber: #F5BA67. Typeface: Open Runde (license included).

All illustrated portfolio values are explicitly labeled example data. Copy intentionally makes no pricing, availability, performance, or real-time guarantees. These assets were created locally and have not been published or uploaded.

## Platform references

- X recommends 1500 × 500 headers and 400 × 400 profile photos: https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/common-issues-when-uploading-profile-photo
- LinkedIn personal background guidance (1584 × 396): https://nonprofit.linkedin.com/blog/2022/04/one-way-to-improve-your-linkedin-profile-as-a-nonprofit-pro
- YouTube recommends 2560 × 1440: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12950272?hl=en ; safe-area guidance: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en-GB
- Facebook and generic feed/story canvases are supplied as reusable layouts; inspect each platform's upload preview for current cropping. A LinkedIn personal cover is not a company-page cover.

YouTube essential artwork fits the centered 1546 × 423 band. Headers keep text away from the lower-left avatar area. Story copy is inset away from top and bottom overlays. Platform previews can still crop differently on different devices.
